Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Snip & Extract is a Windows screenshot and OCR utility developed by MasoomDev.

Screen captures

When you initiate a capture, the app reads the screen area you select. The image is held in the application while you use it. You can choose to save it to a local file or copy it to the Windows clipboard.

Text recognition

OCR uses Windows text-recognition facilities on your device. Recognised text is displayed for editing or copying. The app does not require a MasoomDev account or an external OCR API.

Data collection and sharing

The current app implementation does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, user accounts, or code that uploads captured images or extracted text to MasoomDev servers. If you save or copy content, the destination and any third-party applications you share it with may process it under their own policies.

Storage and retention

Images and text remain in application memory while in use. Saved screenshots persist at the location you select until you remove them. Clipboard content is managed by Windows.

Permissions

Screen capture accesses pixels displayed on your Windows desktop at your request. Windows OCR language support may be required for text extraction.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if app features change. For questions, visit MasoomDev Support.
